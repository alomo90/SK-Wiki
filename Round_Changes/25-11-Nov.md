# Round Changes – November 2025

These are the **round changes only**, not the full bonuses.

---

## Round Era

**Era of Fertility**

* Population increased by **+10%**

---

## Planet Type Changes

### Removed Planet Types

* **Forest & Wilderness** – *Not available*
* **Volcanic Inferno** – *Not available*

---

### Planet Types Changes

**Mysti**

* Hybrid unit cost reduced by **10%**

**Jagged**

* Unit costs reduced by **15%**

**Multiple Terrain**

* Module reset every **12 ticks**
* State change every **12 ticks**
* Race change every **6 ticks**

**Eternal Plains**

* Return time increased by **+5%**

**Shadow**

* Probe losses reduced by **10%**
* **Mine Gold** minimum cost set to **$2 per probe**
* Starts with **Probe Armour II**
* No longer starts with **50 Module Points**

---

## Research Changes

### Probe Armour

Probe Armour has been split into **five separate researches**.

> Reduction applies to **probe losses** and is **total**, not cumulative.

**Probe Armour I**
* **10%** probe loss reduction  
* Cost: **25,000** research points  
* Requires **Fusion Technology**

**Probe Armour II**
* **20%** probe loss reduction  
* Cost: **50,000** research points  
* Requires **Probe Armour I**

**Probe Armour III**
* **30%** probe loss reduction  
* Cost: **75,000** research points  
* Requires **Probe Armour II**

**Probe Armour IV**
* **40%** probe loss reduction  
* Cost: **100,000** research points  
* Requires **Probe Armour III**

**Probe Armour V**
* **50%** probe loss reduction  
* Cost: **125,000** research points  
* Requires **Probe Armour IV**
