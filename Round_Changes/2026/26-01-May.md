# :loudspeaker: Round Changes

## :star2: Round Era – Era of Prosperity
* All kingdoms receive:
  * **+10% income**

## :earth_africa: Planet Type Changes
The following planet types are **disabled this round**:
* Mountainous
* Jagged

## :crossed_swords: Warleader Bonus
* Having **all 4 Warleaders at home**:
  * **+10% income**

## :ear_of_rice: Food Kingdom Changes
Food kingdoms now have **minimum land thresholds**:
* **T2 Food** → minimum **350 land**
* **T3 Food** → minimum **500 land**

Once a food kingdom reaches its minimum land:
* It will **immediately reset**

## Bot Changes
We’ve changed how bot spawning works.

The goal is to:
* Start the universe with **fewer bots** (40, previously 70)
* Let the bot population **self-regulate over time**
* Create **more small bots** instead of too many big bots
* Give smaller kingdoms more food targets
* Give larger kingdoms less free farming and more PvP competition

### :robot: When bot count is very low
If bots make up less than roughly **33% of all kingdoms**, the system will create bots **very frequently**.
In this case, bots can spawn **every tick** until the bot population catches up.

### :earth_africa: Bot land also controls spawn speed
The less total land bots own, the faster new bots spawn.
| Bot-owned land in the universe | Bot spawn rate |
| ------------------------------ | -------------: |
| Less than 10%                  | Every 12 ticks |
| 10% to 20%                     | Every 24 ticks |
| 20% to 30%                     | Every 36 ticks |
| 30% to 40%                     | Every 48 ticks |
| 40% or more                    | Every 60 ticks |

So for example:
* If bots own **less than 10%** of universe land, they spawn every **12 ticks**
* If bots own **50% or more** of universe land, they spawn every **60 ticks**

### :dart: What this means for gameplay
Bots should now grow into the universe more naturally.
There should be more small bots available for smaller kingdoms, while bigger kingdoms should have fewer easy bot targets and more reason to compete with real players.
