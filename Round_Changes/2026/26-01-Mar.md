## :ringed_planet: PT Diversity Changes
The previous diversity bonus has been replaced with a **diversity penalty**.
Each alliance may have:
* **Up to 3 of each planet type** without penalty
For every additional planet of the same type beyond 3:
* **–3% income per extra planet**

### Examples:
* 4 Shadows → **–3%**
* 5 Shadows → **–6%**
* 6 Shadows → **–9%**
* 4 Shadows + 4 VIs → **–6%** (–3% per type exceeding 3)

Penalty stacks per planet type.
