# January Round Changes

This page documents the **changes introduced for the January round**.  
Only **modified values** are listed — unchanged mechanics are not repeated.

---

## Universe Event Changes

### Solar Storm
* Power production reduced by **-50%**  
  *Previous: ~~−25%~~*

---

### Medal of Honor
* **+50% honor**
* **+7% income**  
  *Previous: ~~+20% honor, +3% income~~*

---

### Tranquil Weather Upon Us
* **-40% exploration cost**
* **-20% building cost**  
  *Previous: ~~−20% exploration, −10% building cost~~*

---

### Visit from Unknown Beings
* **+50% research efficiency**  
  *Previous: ~~+25% research efficiency~~*

---

### Hazy Skies Ahead
* **+30% probe damage**  
  *Previous: ~~+15%~~*

---

### Knowledge is Power
* SOKs reveal **scientist and probe counts**
* **Gain 1 Intel Probe Armour per tick**

---

### Offense is the Best Defense
* **+10% offensive strength**  
  *Previous: ~~+5%~~*
* All kingdoms in **Defensive State** are forced into **None State**
* Defensive State is **blocked** for the duration of the event

---

### On the Double
* **Triple** module points per tick  
  *Previous: ~~Double~~*

---

### Solar Flare
* **+50% power production**  
  *Previous: ~~+25%~~*

---

### None the Wiser
* All kingdoms forced into **None State**
* 24-tick cooldown before selecting a new state
* **Event duration reduced to 1 tick**

---

### Shattered Shields
* **-50% shield effectiveness**  
  *Previous: ~~−25%~~*

---

### Malicious Attack
* Attacks ignore **MA protection**
* **Event duration increased to 48 ticks**

---

### Hidden Cache
* Instantly receive **5 million resources**  
  *Previous: ~~2 million~~*
* Receive a **full set of missiles**
* If **Anti-Matter Reactor Core** is not researched:
  * Receive **1/1/1 missiles**
  * Missiles do **not stack**

---

### Battle Scarred
* **-10% balanced unit cost**  
  *Previous: ~~−5%~~*

---

### Caffeine Crusaders
* **-20% return time**  
  *Previous: ~~−10%~~*
* Minimum return time reduced to **5 ticks**

---

### Twice the Shame
* **Triple honor losses**  
  *Previous: ~~Double~~*

---

### Relic Raid
* All relics are captured by **bot alliances**
* **Event duration reduced to 1 tick**

---

## Planet Type Changes

### Unavailable This Round
* **Herbal Jungle**
* **Helium**

---

### Helium (Balance Adjustments)
* **+10% offensive strength**  
  *Previous: ~~+12%~~*
* **+30% research efficiency**  
  *Previous: ~~+25%~~*

---

### Mystical Lands
* Changed to **−5% hybrid unit cost**  
  *Previous: ~~−10% balanced unit cost~~*

---

### Desert Wasteland
* No longer suffers **extra defensive losses**
* **+50% honor gains** added

---

### Shadow Wasteland
* Now ignores **75% MA**  
  *Previous: ~~50%~~*

---

## Gameplay Changes

* You can now **declare war on any alliance with more than 1 member**
* Bots will **spread attacks out**, rather than attacking all targets simultaneously
* Defensive unit disbanding is now capped:
  * **5% of defensive points per tick**, or
  * **5,000 points**, whichever is higher
