# December Round – Christmas Planets

During the December round, a set of **Christmas-themed planet types** was introduced. These planets feature powerful, festive bonuses and unique starting perks.

---

## Mountainoel

**Bonuses**
* +15% defense
* +30% income
* +100% honor bonus

**Penalties**
* None

**Starting Bonuses**
* Starts with **Dragoons** and **Laser Dragoons**

---

## Festive Wilderness

**Bonuses**
* +20% population bonus
* +20 starmine output
* +20% barracks space

**Penalties**
* None

**Starting Bonuses**
* Starts with **Longevity** research
* Population bonus is **always maxed**

---

## Tinsel Form

**Bonuses**
* -65% research required
* +10 maximum research

**Penalties**
* -10% income

**Starting Bonuses**
* Starts with **1500** population, power, military, and money

---

## Mistletoe Lands

**Bonuses**
* -30% return time
* -25% offensive losses
* -15% hybrid unit cost
* Minimum return time: **5 ticks**

**Penalties**
* None

**Starting Bonuses**
* Starts with **Fusion Technology**
* Starts with **EC**
* Starts with **1 Race Token**

---

## Volcandy Cane Inferno

**Bonuses**
* -90% power needed
* -90% shield probe power
* 10× power storage
* +25% probe damage

**Penalties**
* None

**Starting Bonuses**
* Starts with **Fusion Technology**
* Starts with **Large Missiles**

---

## Jolly Tundra

**Bonuses**

**Penalties**
* None

**Starting Bonuses**
* Starts with **Hover Tanks**
